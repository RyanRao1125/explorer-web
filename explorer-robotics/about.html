Explorer Robotics is a Canadian robotics club where young engineers learn to design, build, and compete.

Our Mission

More than a club.
A real engineering experience.

We believe every young person deserves access to hands-on engineering education. At Explorer Robotics, students don't just learn to code, they learn to think critically, work as a team, and solve problems that matter.

From building their first robot to qualifying for national and international competitions, every member is supported every step of the way.

Our Story

Built different, on purpose

There are a lot of robotics programs out there. Franchise clubs, classroom chains, video-based courses. Explorer Robotics was built to be something different.

From day one, the goal was to create a tight-knit community where students actually know their teachers and teammates, not just classmates who happen to share a room. The relationships built here last well beyond a single season.

Every session is hands-on. Students aren't watching videos or following step-by-step kits, they're solving real problems, making real decisions, and building things that actually work. That's why our teams don't just participate at WRO nationals and internationals, they place.

If you're looking for a program where your child is known, challenged, and part of something real, this is it.
