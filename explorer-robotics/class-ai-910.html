About This Class

An advanced project-based course introducing the Engineering Design Process and Systems Engineering Principles with AI tools incorporated throughout. Recommended for students competing in WRO Future Engineer or Future Innovator categories at the senior level.

What Students Will Learn

  • Advanced Engineering Design Process
  • Systems Engineering Principles
  • AI tools in professional workflows
  • Research, analysis, and documentation
  • Solution design and prototyping
  • Presentation and communication of ideas
  • WRO Future Engineer and Future Innovator preparation

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my child need any experience?

No prior experience needed.

What does my child need to bring?

Just themselves. All equipment is provided unless otherwise noted.

Is there a registration deadline?

Spots are limited. We recommend registering early to secure your spot.

What comes after this class?

Students at this level are encouraged to join WRO competition teams and combine this class with Electronics and 3D Printing.
