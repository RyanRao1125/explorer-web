Celebrate 10 Years: Open House on Sunday, August 30

Explorer Robotics is turning 10! Join us for a free Open House celebrating a decade of inspiring future engineers, with 500+ students taught, 15+ international placements, and 30+ national awards along the way. Meet our international competition award teams, catch a keynote on AI and self-driving cars, and let the kids dive into hands-on robot activities, 3D printing, and a Lego Sumo showdown. Free registration is required for build activities: register below or scan the QR code on the poster.

Sunday, August 30 · 1:00 PM to 3:30 PM · 300 Water Street, Whitby ON (Cambridge International Academy)
