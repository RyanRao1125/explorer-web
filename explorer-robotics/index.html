Why Explorer Robotics

Skills you can't get from a textbook

Real engineering

CAD design, mechanical assembly, electrical wiring, and programming. All skills used in actual engineering careers.

Teamwork that matters

Robots don't get built solo. You'll learn to divide work, troubleshoot together, and ship on a deadline.

No experience needed

Most members join knowing nothing about robotics. Mentors and senior members teach everything from scratch.

Competition experience

Travel to regional, national, and even international events. See how other teams build and test your robot under real pressure.

Why Explorer Robotics

Not just another robotics class.

While other programs put kids in front of videos and follow-along kits, we built something different. A real community where students and teachers know each other by name, and every session is spent actually building something.

Join the Club

A tight-knit community

Students build real friendships with teammates and teachers. Relationships that last well beyond a single season.

Hands-on every session

No videos, no passive learning. Every class is spent building, coding, and solving real problems with real tools.

Small groups, big results

We keep classes small so every student gets real attention, and it shows. Our teams place at nationals and internationals.

World Robot Olympiad

Compete on the world stage

Explorer Robotics competes in WRO every year across Future Engineers, RoboMission, and Future Innovators, going up against students from over 85 countries.
